    How to play Frog-Out

    Connection hiccup?

    During a match, we try to restore your frog for up to 30 seconds. Return to the same tab or refresh it; a new tab may not restore you. Your frog stays in play. If someone cannot return in time, everyone returns to the lobby.

    Leave this match?

    Leaving ends the current match for everyone and returns the other players to the lobby.

    For a brief interruption, return to or refresh this same tab instead. We will try to reconnect you for up to 30 seconds.
