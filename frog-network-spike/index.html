    How to Play

    ✦ Chaos Voting — choose the mayhem

    Low gravity. Giant frogs. Spinning platforms. 20 wild modifiers make every match a new kind of mischief.

    • Round 1 is normal. Between rounds, when another round remains, you have 15 seconds to vote for the next twist.
    • Everyone, including CPUs, gets one vote. Most votes wins; visible tie priority settles ties.
    • Up to 3 effects stack. The oldest cycles out when a new one wins — or vote KEEP to renew it as the newest.

    Chaos is on by default. The host can turn it off in the lobby. Effects reset for a new match; your selected game mode’s rules and scoring still apply.

    Movement and Controls

    • WASD / Arrow keys: move.
    • Aim: with Left Click, point the mouse (360°). With Space, aim with WASD / arrows.
    • Left Click or Space: tap/release to jump; hold grounded, then release for a charged jump.
    • In the air, press to fire your tongue. Hold when attached to reel; release to detach and keep momentum.
    • Esc — Open / Close Settings (Music & Volume).
    • Left pad: move + aim freely. Touch anywhere in the left movement area, then drag.
    • Right action area: touch anywhere to place the JUMP button; tap/release to jump. Hold to charge; release to launch.
    • In the air, press the TONGUE button to fire.
    • Hold when attached to pull; release to detach and keep your momentum.
    • ⚙ Settings: music and volume, on every screen.

    Game Modes

    Poison Tag rules

    One Poison Dart Frog starts the chase. Frog-body contact spreads poison; tongues never do. Newly poisoned frogs transform for 1 second before they can spread it.

    Safe frogs earn 1 point/second, plus 10 points for being last safe. The round ends when everyone is poisoned. Same-time last survivors share the bonus.

    Everyone starts as Poison Dart Frog once. Highest total wins; ties share victory.

    Freeze Tag rules

    One freezer tags bodies to freeze frogs. Unfrozen runners touch frozen teammates to rescue them. Tongues never freeze or rescue. No automatic thaw.

    60 seconds, or until all runners freeze. With two players, the first freeze ends the round. Rescue grants 1 second of protection.

    Runners: 1 point/second unfrozen, plus 5 points/rescue and 8 bonus points if unfrozen at round end. Freezer: 3 points/freeze + 20 bonus points when all runners are frozen at once. Everyone starts freezer once. Highest total wins; ties share victory.

    Classic Tag rules

    One frog is IT. Body contact passes IT; tongues never tag. New IT cannot tag for 1 second.

    60 seconds. Earn 1 point/second not IT. Everyone starts IT once. Highest total wins; ties share victory.

    Winning and Scoring

    Each player starts as the Poison Dart Frog, freezer, or IT once per match. Points add up across rounds. Highest total wins; tied totals share victory.

    The mode descriptions above explain round endings and points. Round results show your score changes; the last round goes straight to the final standings.

    Connection hiccup?

    During a match, we try to restore your frog for up to 30 seconds. Return to the same tab or refresh it; a new tab may not restore you. Your frog stays visible as OFFLINE and its score pauses. If the window expires, only your spot is removed; the room stays open.

    Leave this match?

    Leaving removes only your frog. The other players continue. If you are the tagger/freezer, the round advances; if only one player remains, they return to the lobby.

    Leave is permanent for this match. For a brief interruption, refresh or return to this same tab within 30 seconds instead.

    Settings

    On supported phones, Back opens settings, then closes it. A further Back leaves the site. The ⚙ button always works.
