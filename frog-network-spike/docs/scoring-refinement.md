# Scoring refinement

Baseline: c55a052bf84ffcbcc263db6b0609679d420c45cf, checkpoint/approved-wind-pogo-bubble-refinements.

Poison Tag retains survival scoring and tied last-safe victories; last-safe bonus increases from 2 to 10.
Freeze Tag retains 1 point per unfrozen second, plus 5 per successful rescue and 8 if present and unfrozen when an active round ends. The freezer retains 3 per freeze and receives 20 when all remaining runners are frozen concurrently (including two-player rooms); separate freeze contacts can complete the clear. Rescued frogs may be frozen again under the unchanged protection rules. End bonuses lock at settlement; countdown departures earn no unfrozen bonus.
Classic Tag is unchanged. No input, physics, CPU, arena, modifier, sound or networking behavior changes.
